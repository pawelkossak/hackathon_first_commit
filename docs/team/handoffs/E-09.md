# E-09: plan matcher, account clock and future-script replay

## Built
- `engine/matcher.ts` (new). `index.ts` `advanceAccountClock` only delegates to `replayAccount`.
  - **Replay.** Rows of `data/future-script.json` dated after the old account clock and up to the target date (default 5 XII) are booked on the synthetic account: L01 25 XI −4 300, L02 30 XI +7 380, and the 3 XII +15 000 from Klient A when C1 is played in the current lap **or** its plan is saved (a saved plan survives a rewind). They are appended to `account.matchedTx` as `Transaction`s, and `account.balance` follows: 56 080 with C1, 41 080 without.
  - **Matcher** `matchPlan(plan, transactions, clock)`. Only transactions in the plan's direction, dated ≤ deadline and ≤ clock count (ARCHITECTURE §4):
    - `met`: counterparty matches and |amount| is inside amount ± tolerancePct % (integer arithmetic);
    - `open`: no full match and the deadline is after the account clock;
    - `ask`: deadline reached, a transaction matches only the counterparty or only the amount;
    - `missed`: deadline reached, nothing matches (variant B, `futureScript.variantB`).
  - **Validation.** Every saved plan is checked again on each advance. A plan that becomes `met` calls `coreAfterValidation(core, cardId)` (C1 → Luka płynnościowa 3) and emits `planValidated { week: core.week, planId }`. A plan that is already met does not emit again.
  - **Never touched:** game week, status, stars, unlock, snapshots, hand, ledger. The game clock (`resolveWeek`, `rewind`) never touches the account. Reset clears both clocks.
  - A target date before the account clock, or an invalid date, throws `invalid_date` (unchanged). The same date is allowed: it re-checks plans saved after the last advance.
- `data.ts` load checks for the future script: replay rows are strictly after the account start and in date order; a ledger row has a known `ledgerId` and matches the persona ledger (date, amount, counterparty); a card row has a known `cardId`; the default target is not before the start.
