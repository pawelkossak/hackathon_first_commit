# E-05: turn state machine, snapshots, rewind and card combinations

## Built
- `engine/turn.ts` (new) holds the turn state machine. `index.ts` `resolveWeek` and `rewind` now only delegate to it. Each week runs like this:
  1. The week starts and its snapshot is taken.
  2. The shown event is resolved with `resolveEvent`.
  3. The player may play one card (`playCard`).
  4. `resolveWeek` settles the week, then one of three things happens:
     - **crash**: status `crashed`, every star `missed`, and the lap stops.
     - **week 7**: status `completed`, the lap score is set, and Pilot is `pending` or `missed`.
     - **otherwise**: the next week starts, gets its snapshot, and its shown event emits its signal.
- `settleWeek` is the old `resolveWeek` logic with these additions:
  - A guard so a week settles only once (`invalid_week`).
  - `core.lowestBalance` is now updated after every settled week. It is the lowest end-of-day balance from 23 XI to the end of that week, and the field doc already said "so far". At lap end it equals `forecast.low`.
- `rewindTo`:
  - Works from any status (`playing`, `crashed`, `completed`).
  - Checks the requested week: a week outside 1–7 gives `invalid_week`. A missing snapshot, or one whose `week` does not match, gives `no_snapshot`.
  - Restores the snapshot `GameCore` (no nested snapshots) and drops the snapshots of later weeks.
  - Keeps `account` (clock and saved plans).
  - Emits the restored week's shown-event signals again: E2 `bigProject`, E5 `loanOffered`, E6 `lateClient`. It emits no `lapStart`, because a rewind is not a new lap.
- **Pilot decision (QA P2 from E-01):**
  - Pilot lives in `core.stars`.
  - It becomes `pending` only at lap end (week 7, lowest own balance ≥ 0), otherwise `missed`.
  - `savePlan` makes Pilot `earned` only when the lap is `completed` and Pilot is `pending` (`coreAfterPlanSave`). In every other case it leaves Pilot as it is: `open` mid-lap, `missed` after a crash or a low below 0. It also refreshes `level2Unlocked`.
  - A plan saved mid-lap therefore leaves Pilot `open`, and lap end sets it to `pending` anyway. **This replaces the E-03 behaviour**, where a plan saved before lap end auto-earned Pilot. The player saves the winning decision after the lap (G18 → G22), and the returned state must be persisted before success is shown, as the contract requires.
  - Rewind drops Pilot together with the core. The saved plan survives in `account.plans`, and a replayed lap end is `pending` again.
  - `lapScore` keeps its `planSaved` parameter. It is still used by ET-C07 and ET-C09, and `resolveWeek` always passes `false`.
- Card combinations needed no engine change. The existing card overlay in `forecast.ts` and the rules in `cards.ts` already produce every ET-X value.

## API changes
No signature changes, and no new engine event types. Behaviour changes:
- `resolveWeek` at lap end no longer earns Pilot from a plan saved earlier. Pilot is `pending` until `savePlan` is called after the lap.
- `savePlan` mid-lap keeps Pilot `open`.
- `rewind` throws `invalid_week` for a week outside 1–7.
- `core.lowestBalance` is filled after each settled week, instead of only at lap end.
