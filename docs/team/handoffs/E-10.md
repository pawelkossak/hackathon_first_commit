# E-10: demo path end to end, ET inventory, rewind keeps competence 3, one fraud-reveal rule

## Built
- **`engine/demo.test.ts` (new).**
  - ET-D01 drives the engine only through `engine/index.ts`, step by step through GAME-DESIGN §9. After every transition it checks two things: the input was not mutated, and the output survives a JSON round trip.
  - It asserts every number of §9 and §7.8 as spec literals, plus the narration events listed in the table below.
- **ET inventory (same file).** At test time it reads `ENGINE-DATA-AND-TESTS.md` §7 with `node:fs`. It checks:
  - the spec lists **58** distinct ET ids, which matches the brief;
  - every id is the title prefix of at least one engine `it(...)`;
  - no test names an unknown id.

  ET-F01 has three tests (from E-02), so the check is "at least one", not "exactly one".
- **Coordinator decision a: rewind keeps competence 3.**
  - `rewindTo` (`engine/turn.ts`) now re-applies `coreAfterValidation` for every `met` plan in `account.plans`. It does this on the restored core and on the kept snapshots. The step is idempotent.
  - So Luka płynnościowa stays 3 after any rewind and in a replayed lap.
  - `resetGame` clears the account, and the competence goes with it.
- **Coordinator decision b: one fraud-reveal rule (QA P2).** Both new functions are in `engine/forecast.ts`.
  - `fraudRevealsOf(core, points, crash)` runs over the ground-truth series. A resolved `reveal` choice is reported only if its payment was really booked to the changed account. That means an own day (not counterfactual), and the payment is not the rejected crash transaction.
  - The report date is the later of the data reveal date (23 XII) and the payment date.
  - A crash before that date, or a report date outside the lap, means no report.
  - `revealedFrauds(core)` keeps the reveals whose report week is in `core.weeksResolved`. That is exactly the condition under which `resolveWeek` emitted `fraudRevealed`.
  - `turn.ts` `fraudSignals` uses `fraudRevealsOf`. The player-facing overlay uses `revealedFrauds` through the new internal `OverlayContext.revealed` field, which replaces the old "23 XII < current week start" test.
  - The two paths now behave like this:
    - **Demo/23 XII path:** unchanged. The signal comes in the T5 resolution and the views show the fraud from T6. ET-E01, E02 and E03 are unchanged.
    - **C3 + pay_as_is (C3 alone):** L08 moves to 8 I and is rejected by the 8 I crash (ET-C06). The engine emits no `fraudRevealed` and the views never show E1. Before this change, the views showed it from T6.
    - **C1 + C3 (T3) + pay_as_is:** the payment goes out on 8 I. The T7 resolution emits `fraudRevealed {week 7, date 2027-01-08, amount −35 700}` and then `crash` (8 I at E1, short 25 715). The views hide the fraud in T5–T7 and show it after that resolution.

## Bugs found by the end-to-end run
None. The demo path passed through the API unchanged. The only changes to the engine are the two coordinator decisions above.

## API changes
- No public signature changes and no new exported functions in `index.ts`.
- New internal helpers: `fraudRevealsOf`, `revealedFrauds` and `FraudReveal` in `forecast.ts`. `OverlayContext` gains `revealed`.
- Behaviour changes:
  - `rewind` keeps competence 3 of met plans;
  - on the C3 + `pay_as_is` paths the views and `fraudRevealed` follow the payment date (see above);
  - `fraudRevealed.date` is 8 I on the C1 + C3 path.
- The `forecast` doc comment is updated.

## Open points
1. On the C1 + C3 + `pay_as_is` path the report day (8 I) is also the day of the E1 re-payment, which is fixed at 8 I in the data. The re-payment then crashes the lap in the same resolution. If design wants the re-payment to follow the report, that is a data/spec change.
2. `revealedFrauds` runs one extra ground-truth series in the player views, and only when a `reveal` choice is resolved. The cost is negligible.
