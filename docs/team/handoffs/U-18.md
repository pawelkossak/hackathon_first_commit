## Built
- **Level 2 Page (`app/gra/2/page.tsx`):**
  - Integrated mission *„Poziom 2: Pierwszy pracownik (Luty 2027)”*.
  - Lock mechanism: when Level 1 has not been completed with ≥ 1 star, displays fog lock screen (`data-testid="level2-locked-screen"`) with explanation and link to campaign map.
  - Active game screen (`data-testid="level2-active-screen"`):
    - Level 2 HUD: running cash balance, week counter (1..3), stars indicator, and ledger history.
    - Week 1: Recruitment decision — Zatrudnij pracownika (-6 000 zł 10 II, -1 200 zł 15 II) vs Odrocz zatrudnienie.
    - Week 2: Invoice timing — Pobierz zaliczkę 3 000 zł (8 II) vs Czekaj na pełną fakturę (12 II).
    - Week 3: Tax reserve — Utwórz rezerwę 4 300 zł vs Wspólne saldo bez rezerwy.
  - Failure & Rewind: Hire + Wait triggers crash on 10 Feb with 900 PLN shortfall; one-click rewind restores Week 2.
  - Educational success states:
    - Hire + Advance + Reserve: wins with 1 500 PLN and 3 stars.
    - Defer + Wait + Unreserved: wins with 8 700 PLN and 2 stars.
- **State Store (`lib/ui/level2-store.ts`):**
  - Implements canonical simulation and pure computation functions (`computeLevel2`).
  - Persists choices in `localStorage` under `pilot.employee.v1` key; cleared on mission reset.

  - Verified lock/unlock gates, 10 Feb 900 PLN shortfall crash, rewind recovery, 3-star hire path, and 2-star deferral path.
