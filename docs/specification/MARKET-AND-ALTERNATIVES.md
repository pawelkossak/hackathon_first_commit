**Conclusion for positioning:** scenario tools exist (Agicap, Qonto) but are built for finance teams or are not in Poland. Polish bank and accounting tools stop at balances, reminders and a forecast at most. Nothing we found joins **simulation on own data → checkable plan → validation on the account → lesson** for a micro-entrepreneur, inside a bank. That loop is our difference, not the forecast. Jury line: *"mOrganizer tells you what will happen. Agicap is for a CFO. We teach the owner who has no CFO."*

## 3. Bank business case — KPI hypotheses (G-12)

Values are **hypotheses for a pilot**, not facts; to be set by Alan and checked with mentors (Q-10).

| KPI | Proves | Pilot hypothesis (6 months) |
|---|---|---|
| Plans met on the account / plans saved | behaviour change (continuously improve) | ≥ 40 % |
| Simulations before a big decision (hire, contract > 50 % annual revenue) | safe training ground is used | ≥ 1 per decision for active users |
| Weekly active users among SME clients offered the feature | "actively seek it out" | 15–20 % |
| Return after a gap alert within 48 h | trigger works | ≥ 50 % |
| Days with negative balance per client vs control group | lower portfolio risk | −20 % |
| Share of expensive non-bank loans after a simulated gap | lower cost of crises | falling trend |
| Product take-up after a simulation (overdraft, factoring, leasing) | contextual banking | uplift vs banner campaigns; never a reward in the app |
| Advisor meetings started from a simulator scenario | better advisor talks | tracked from day 1 |

Pitch: name 3 at most — plans met, fewer negative-balance days, return after alert.
