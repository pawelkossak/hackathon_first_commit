# Client expectations — Finance Quest

The "client" is the challenge owner: Commerzbank (Digital Technology Centre Poland, Innovation Lab Frankfurt). The project owner is Alan Bartczak.

Challenge (EN, as in the concept): *"How might we make financial learning so engaging and relevant that clients/entrepreneurs actively seek it out and continuously improve their financial decision-making?"*

| Id | Expectation | Origin | How we prove it in the demo |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-01 | **Financial learning** — the user actually learns finance concepts | challenge wording | Competence map grows from actions; "avoided cost of mistake" |
| E-02 | **Engaging** — the experience pulls, not pushes | challenge wording | Live chart reacting < 200 ms to a slider; emergency drill cards |
| E-03 | **Relevant** — on the entrepreneur's own situation | challenge wording | Every number comes from the persona's own (synthetic) account |
| E-04 | **Actively seek it out** — users come back unprompted | challenge wording | Two triggers: fear (gap alert) and ambition ("can I afford…") |
| E-05 | **Continuously improve** decision-making | challenge wording | Loop: decision → action plan → validation on account → lesson; time-travel button |
| E-06 | Target user: **entrepreneurs / SME clients** of a bank | challenge wording | Persona Ania, sole proprietor, graphic studio |
| E-07 | Fits the event theme **"Innovations for all generations"** | event brief | Open — see GAPS G-01 |
| E-08 | Based on a **real business need** of the bank; may be developed after the event | event brief | Bank value slide; integration path with mBank (Commerzbank group) tools |
| E-09 | Built and presented by a team of 3-6 in **48 h** | event brief | Working prototype on synthetic data + 3-min pitch |
| E-10 | Judging criteria | not published | Open — see QUESTIONS Q-01; assume usual: problem fit, innovation, feasibility, business value, demo quality |

Implicit expectations (owner concept, §11, §13): not "yet another cash-flow tool"; not a hidden sales funnel; AI must not invent numbers; honest separation of demo vs vision.
